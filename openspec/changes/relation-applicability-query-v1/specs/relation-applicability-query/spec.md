## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pair scores are independent of relation bounds
The study SHALL use the fixed 0.50 semantic, 0.20 BM25, 0.20 resolved symbol/path, 0.10 literal-condition formula, with A0/R/V/S/V-no-cost ordering and original solver/fallback.
#### Scenario: Unknown pair receives low score
- **WHEN** a score or identifier changes
- **THEN** only ordering and genuine ties can change; unknown bounds and accepted labels remain intact

### Requirement: Acquisition has measured costs and isolated contexts
The study SHALL measure cold/reused process and batch 2/8 configurations on identical eight development pairs twice. Every batch SHALL use a fresh empty thread. Starts SHALL require estimated time times 1.25 plus five seconds within remaining time. Timeouts SHALL remain censored observations and all costs SHALL be retained.
#### Scenario: Remaining time is 2.7 seconds
- **WHEN** comparable batch cost is fifteen seconds
- **THEN** no request starts and the current package returns with NO_AFFORDABLE_BATCH

### Requirement: Finite frozen study and independent source audit
The study SHALL freeze four existing public states and at most 48 stratified pairs each before labels; freeze implementation before two check states; conduct sealed incremental Q replay and twelve independent 60-second A0/R/V acquisitions; blind J to Q and method information. Missing/unknown/disputed/unsampled results SHALL stay in denominators.
#### Scenario: Reviewer disagrees or model fails
- **WHEN** J disputes Q or a response is missing
- **THEN** the record persists without resampling valid semantic results or inventing labels

### Requirement: Restricted execution and honest reporting
The CLI SHALL only prepare, benchmark, build reference, replay, acquire, review and summarize relations. It SHALL preserve legacy evidence and defaults and output four tables plus reproduction commands and a Chinese account.
#### Scenario: Relation retrieval improves
- **WHEN** supported relations or package changes are observed
- **THEN** functional_repair_gain remains NOT_TESTED_THIS_STAGE and no repair agent, training or target acceptance starts
