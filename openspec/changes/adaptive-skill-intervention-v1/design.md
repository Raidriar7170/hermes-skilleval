## Risks / Trade-offs

- Forked history does not establish identical hidden randomness → claim observable-prefix matching only.
- Turn-boundary pausing is coarser than individual events → record detected events and actual completed boundary.
- Small/noisy data → retain all outcomes, compare zero/prior/task baselines, avoid gain claims unsupported by the matrix.
- Credentials and hidden checks → reuse isolated transport; never mount checks or source credentials into tool-visible paths.
