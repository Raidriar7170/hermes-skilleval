## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete public state
The study SHALL preserve full request spans and conditions, relate only checkpoint-visible observations, and assign zero functional weight to unrelated environment failures.

#### Scenario: Historical failure and later limited pass
- **WHEN** a public prefix contains environment failures, a limited passing check and later source changes
- **THEN** corresponding histories remain visible, unrelated obligations remain unverified, and stale support cannot certify changed source.

### Requirement: Full legal index and common retrieval
The study SHALL index legal exact-base source, documents and existing public tests before task filtering, retain long definitions as metadata, and supply identical candidates and payload budgets to all selectors.

#### Scenario: Late source symbol
- **WHEN** relevant code is beyond the old forty-unit subset
- **THEN** global lexical/symbol retrieval can find it and bounded real-edge expansion records its provenance.

### Requirement: Grounded relation coverage
The study SHALL distinguish documented contracts, existing behavior and public examples, validate relation spans and applicability, and keep blinded independent source review separate from selection scores.

#### Scenario: Same words in unrelated code
- **WHEN** a candidate shares words but supplies no grounded support
- **THEN** it receives TOPICAL_ONLY or UNRESOLVED with zero relation coverage.

### Requirement: Fixed attributable functional study
The study SHALL freeze up to three source-ordered eligible new states, one prefix each and two tails per N/M-local/H-sim/H-link, preserve all attempts, and judge target and protected outcomes independently of coverage scores.

#### Scenario: Unknown target and trusted protection failure
- **WHEN** candidate integrity is established and a trusted in-scope protection check fails while target classification is unknown
- **THEN** the new protocol reports functional FAIL while preserving both component outcomes and all legacy report semantics.

### Requirement: Conservative delivery
The study SHALL report representation, source relations, selection and functional effects separately, retain unknown denominators, preserve historical evidence and default policy, and publish only a sanitized Draft PR.

#### Scenario: No measured gain
- **WHEN** H-link does not outperform common-pool MMR
- **THEN** the report states gain not established and does not promote or expand the study.
