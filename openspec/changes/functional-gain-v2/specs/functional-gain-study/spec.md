## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pure functional objective
Data construction, training and evaluation SHALL load the objective lock and derive functionality only from valid target and protected regression verdicts under intact verification.
#### Scenario: Ordinary policy rejection
- **WHEN** target and regression pass but a documentation edit violates file policy
- **THEN** functionality is one, qualified delivery is zero and policy cannot change gain or wait targets.
#### Scenario: Missing evidence
- **WHEN** either functional check is invalid or absent
- **THEN** functionality and paired gain remain UNKNOWN with the planned denominator preserved.

### Requirement: Matched-state mechanisms
Representation ablations SHALL use identical prefixes, candidates, actual payloads and remaining budgets. Myopic SHALL reuse full gain weights. Delayed branches SHALL execute forward from the same starting checkpoint without borrowing another branch's future.
#### Scenario: Positive gain delayed
- **WHEN** positive current gain is no larger than positive wait value
- **THEN** full defers, myopic injects, and mechanism value requires actual paired functional outcomes.

### Requirement: Frozen research and honest claims
The study SHALL follow Goal sections 7–30 for source qualification, splits, repeats, training, final sealing and separate completion/claim states; legacy results and defaults SHALL be preserved.
#### Scenario: All functional outcomes tie
- **WHEN** all valid paired functional differences are zero
- **THEN** neither policy improvements nor lower cost produces a functional-gain claim or a claim of learned rescue.
