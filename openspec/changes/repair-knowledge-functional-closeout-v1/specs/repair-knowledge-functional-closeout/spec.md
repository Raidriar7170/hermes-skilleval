## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Frozen public eligibility and matched execution
The study SHALL freeze a finite public-mechanism roster before sampling, use one public prefix per task and two N/M/R tails per task, and preserve all planned cells including identical packages and fallbacks.

#### Scenario: Same-package task
- **WHEN** R and M select the same content
- **THEN** both methods remain in the main functional and cost tables.

### Requirement: Existing R identity and independent relations
R SHALL retain PR #55 weighted pair query ordering and fixed batch startup guard, followed by the existing relation store and pack selector, with no reference Q/J preload and an empty store for each repeat.

#### Scenario: Auxiliary acquisition fails
- **WHEN** R cannot acquire usable relations within its remaining budget
- **THEN** it uses legal MMR fallback while retaining its complete cost and trace, independently of N/M availability.

### Requirement: Own-cost budget and original capture
Every method SHALL pay its own online preparation within 600 seconds including the common prefix; no repeat or fallback SHALL refresh that budget. Candidate reconstruction SHALL preserve the original complete capture and mode sidecar.

#### Scenario: Interrupted attempt
- **WHEN** a started cell cannot resume its original state and remaining time
- **THEN** the cell remains unknown with the original attempt retained and no resampling.

### Requirement: Functional evidence and bounded reporting
Functional labels SHALL depend on candidate identity and valid target/protected checks, separately from policy, budget and protocol fields. Replay SHALL make no model or acceptance calls. Reports SHALL include all planned denominators and unknown bounds.

#### Scenario: Missing evidence
- **WHEN** candidate identity or necessary checks cannot be confirmed
- **THEN** report UNKNOWN without imputing success or failure.
