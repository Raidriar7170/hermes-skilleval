## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Same-library bounded native comparison
The study SHALL follow the activated Phase 2 Goal: four new tasks across two repositories, two independent repeats per arm, shared skill assets and memory generation/use disabled. ASSIST SHALL add only original metadata and paths from fixed MiniLM/MMR; all calls and unknowns SHALL remain recorded.

#### Scenario: Formal comparison
- **WHEN** material, environment qualification, security and timing preflights are frozen
- **THEN** sixteen independent repairs execute without result-driven replacement and hidden acceptance is deferred until all calls finish

### Requirement: Complete timing and isolated assets
The parent SHALL start timing before task reads and worker dispatch, enforce one 900-second deadline including capture, and preserve exceptional terminal records. System skill assets SHALL be readable while credentials and hidden answers remain denied.

#### Scenario: Preparation fails or runs out of time
- **WHEN** any online stage raises or exhausts the shared deadline
- **THEN** the attempt retains elapsed time, actual start status and capture status without resetting the budget or deleting its denominator

### Requirement: Truthful independent acceptance and delivery
All original candidates SHALL be reconstructed at base and checked using author target/protected tests. Results SHALL preserve PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN, raw counts, cost and visible usage limitations and end at a Draft PR without policy promotion.

#### Scenario: No navigation advantage
- **WHEN** results tie, fail or lack skill reads
- **THEN** the study completes its frozen denominator and reports the evidence without extra repair samples or new training
