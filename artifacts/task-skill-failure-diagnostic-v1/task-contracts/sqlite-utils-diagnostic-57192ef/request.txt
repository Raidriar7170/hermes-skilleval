Preserve index name, uniqueness, column order, descending flags and collations, including swapped names and names shared by table/column/index. Do not cascade textual substitutions. Partial or expression indexes affected by a rename must raise TransformError before changing schema or data; dropping an indexed column without handling its index must continue to fail safely.
